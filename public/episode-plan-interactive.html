Life After UI: What designers should practice next

AI speeds up production, but selling the idea remains the designer’s most valuable skill. This episode maps the shift from shipping screens to making better decisions with AI.

The real bottleneck

Faster tools don’t remove judgment. The conversation turns on why taste, framing, and trust become the work as interfaces disappear.

A practical takeaway

Leave with a short checklist for evaluating AI-assisted work: what to automate, what to own, and how to explain the decision.
